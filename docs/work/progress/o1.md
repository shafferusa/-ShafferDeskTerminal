## Done
- equity_option: spec in test/matrix/specs/option.mjs (buy to open, increase, part sale, early exercise by hand, written
  uncovered in a second strategy instance with the long still held (gross), reserve marked at end of day, withdrawal of
  reserved cash refused, buy to close, expiry item blocked until the close exists, automatic exercise in the money with
  delivery at the strike, trade after expiry refused, shares sold). Passes at engine, API and browser level.
- Browser driver test/matrix/drivers/browser/option.mjs: contractTerms (registration form), ticket (contract found from the
  underlying's Options and futures tab, else its Marketplace), actions.lifecycle (Exercise / Early assignment dialogs).
- Terminal fix: server/products/option.js, the option.delivery event now says "Simulated delivery: received/delivered ..."
  (it did not say the delivery is simulated). Found by equity_option:exercise-early.

- etf_option: written cash-secured puts (reserve, purchase with reserved cash refused), covered call through the
  Strategies page, exercise of a written option refused, early assignment by hand, Good Friday (3 April 2026) carried,
  roll of the written puts to May 78 (net credit), automatic assignment of the calls at April expiry, May puts lapse.
  Passes at engine, API and browser level.
- Harness (shared, additive): lib/actions.mjs has a `roll` step (TRADE_ACTIONS + previewFor); lib/normalize.mjs adds
  `netPremium` and `optionRequirement` to the normalized preview. My driver has actions.roll and actions.package
  (Strategies page, driven by `strategyPage` on the step). README row for `roll` still to add.

- option_on_future: calls on a 1,000-barrel crude future bought, puts written (reserve 132,000), early exercise into a
  future at the strike with 6,500 margin, variation margin at end of day, future closed, part sale, expiry (calls
  exercised into 2 futures, puts lapse), futures closed. Passes at engine, API and browser level.
- Terminal fixes found by option_on_future (regression tests in test/core/option-on-future.test.js, 5 tests):
  * server/products/option.js: notional of an option on a future left out the future's multiplier (284.84 for 284,840);
    deliverable units now default to 1 future (not "the multiplier") when the underlying is a future; new export
    underlyingUnits(app, inst).
  * server/core/payoff.js (shared core, 2 small edits): optionRequirement and expiryPayoff measured an option on a
    future in futures contracts, not barrels: a written put on a 1,000-barrel future reserved 132 instead of 132,000.
  * web/lib/contracts.js: hint of "Deliverable units per contract" now says it defaults to 1 future for an option on a future.

- index_option: European cash-settled, 100 x index, AM-settled. Account buys calls and writes an uncovered call (reserve
  20% of 100 x index, marked at end of day); Treasury buys puts; early exercise and early assignment refused (Account
  and Treasury); part sale; expiry items blocked until the settlement value is entered by hand on the index reference
  instrument; cash settlement (exercise and assignment) and lapse. Passes at engine, API and browser level.
  The index level is carried by a reference instrument registered as an ETF (no index product in the catalog): recorded
  in $SP/BUGS.md.

- listed_option: EUR put on a Xetra share (Eurex, TARGET calendar) in a USD Book: protective put package from the
  Strategies page, Easter closing days carried (T+2 shares settle 7 April), EUR/USD 1.10 -> 1.12 with FX effect checked,
  2-for-1 split adjusts the contract (10 contracts at 20), Close half on the contract ticket (actions.close in my
  driver), automatic exercise delivering 500 shares at 20, hedge.review alert, shares sold. Passes at all three levels.
