## Decisions and findings to report
- No ledger accrual for swaps between payment dates: the mark (NPV per 100) is the whole value including accrued. Stated in
  the spec header and asserted (no accrued lines). To be reported as a design point, not changed.
- Preview/strategy leg labels say "Buy/Sell N <swap>" (server/core/packages.js legLabel, describeOrder): wording against the
  "never buy/sell" rule; history summaries fixed in swap.js; labels left (shared core + browser harness regexp) -> BUGS.md.
- Catalog has no "reference index" product for inflation legs (CPI must be registered as a stand-in instrument).
- Increase mid-period takes the whole period on the added notional; a position first opened mid-period pays from its
  trade date with that date's fixing (cashflowsFrom): inconsistent pair of rules, documented, not changed.
