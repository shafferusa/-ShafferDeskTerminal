## Done
- Engine (server/products/option.js, OTC branch only; listed option suite re-run green):
  - independent amount only on a WRITTEN OTC option (collateralNotional = |qty| x strike x multiplier when short, 0 when long);
  - OTC notional = qty x multiplier x underlying price (was qty x deliverable units x price: wrong for bond options);
  - OTC deliverable units default = multiplier / (underlying multiplier x 0.01 for a bond), stated figure overrides;
  - Bermudan exercise style (OTC only) with terms.exerciseDates; exercise/assign allowed on those dates or at expiry;
  - physical FX exercise exchanges both currencies only when the paying currency is available (refused / expiry item fails and retries otherwise), labelled "Simulated currency exchange"; bond delivery summary says "plus accrued interest".
- web/lib/contracts.js F.otcoption: Bermudan option, "Bermudan exercise dates", "Deliverable units per unit of quantity".
- Browser driver test/matrix/drivers/browser/otcoption.mjs (contractTerms, ticket, actions reusing swap's agreement/close/resize/owner_screens/manual_rate and option's exercise/assign, plus barrier event).
- otc_option spec passes engine/API/browser.
- bond_option (Treasury-owned; Bermudan physical call delivered at strike plus accrued; written European cash put ITM) passes engine/API/browser.
- fx_option (EUR/JPY, JPY premium, USD reporting and USD CSA; physical exchange fails visibly until Treasury funds the yen) passes engine/API/browser.
- Fix server/api.js refreshBook: FX refresh throttle keyed by the set of pairs, so a currency newly in the Book gets a current rate (found by fx_option:fund-yen-then-exchange at API level: NAV provisional, EUR stale).
- commodity_option (WTI, two options in one Account netting set, early assignment with simulated delivery of barrels, cash settlement ITM) passes engine/API/browser.
- crypto_option (24x7 calendar: Saturday trades, Sunday settlement and expiry; physical coins on early exercise) passes engine/API/browser. Collateral VM runs on New York business days only (stated in the spec).
- web/views/registry.js CalendarField now offers ALLDAYS (the form could not give a crypto option the 24x7 calendar; found by crypto_option browser setup).
- digital_option (fixed payout ITM paid across Juneteenth; written digital with fixed IA = max payout lapses) passes engine/API/browser.
- interest_rate_option (Term SOFR caplet/floorlet, multiplier = dcf/100, fixing entered by hand, paid in arrears on terms.paymentDate) passes engine/API/browser; rate options take notional = quantity.
- swaption (payer physical: expiry asks for the decision, exercised by hand into the registered forward-starting IRS at a stated entry price, swap then terminated; receiver cash: settlement amount entered on the Pending tab) passes engine/API/browser.
- barrier_option (otcoption-b.mjs: knock-in and knock-out recorded by hand, second record refused (api.js barrier case now refuses a repeat), rebate paid at expiry) passes engine/API/browser.
- asian_option (five averaging closes, three supplied, two entered by hand; expiry blocked naming missing dates) passes engine/API/browser.
- Fix server/data/index.js closeFor: a price entered today as the close of another date is not today's close (found by asian_option:fixing-27-may-by-hand); regression test in test/core/otcoption.test.js.
- lookback_option (fixed-strike call: maximum recorded by hand, replaced; floating-strike put waits for its maximum; early exercise of cash lookback refused) passes engine/API/browser.
- basket_option (Treasury-owned; basket level entered by hand on each expiry item) passes engine/API/browser.
- spread_option (crack spread value entered by hand; Account netting set under one CSA) passes engine/API/browser.
- quanto_option (Tokyo stock, USD payoff at the fixed 0.0068, not the market 1/150) passes engine/API/browser.
- Harness: test/matrix/lib/specs.mjs accepts the product's instrument registered by a register_instrument step (contract naming an agreement).
- test/core/otcoption.test.js: 4 tests (writer-only IA, Bermudan, FX funded exchange, new currency rate refresh); passes.
