Run one product: `SDT_MATRIX_RESULTS=$SP/b1/results SDT_MATRIX_TMP=$SP/b1/tmp SDT_MATRIX_PRODUCT=<id> node --disable-warning=ExperimentalWarning --test test/matrix/engine.test.js`,
then `npm run -s test:api -- --product=<id>` and `npm run -s test:browser -- --product=<id>` with the same two variables.

## Done
- `test/matrix/specs/bond-government.mjs`: header with the bond rules; `treasury_note` (14 steps + 2 refusals) passes at engine, API and browser.
- `test/matrix/drivers/browser/bond.mjs`: registration (all bond contract fields, calendars, lag, primary view, exchange/OTC,
  settlement currency) and the bond ticket (incl. Settlement field, short borrow assumption, financing). Header lists what it supports.
- Terminal fixes in `server/products/bond.js` (all found by treasury_note):
  1. accrued trued DOWN to the trade date at end of day (negative coupon income on trade date): accrual target is now
     interest on settled face to D + interest in unsettled trades to their settlement dates (`accrualTarget`).
  2. after downtime the end-of-day run of an earlier date re-accrued the old period after the coupon was paid:
     position keeps `data.accruedThrough`; older end-of-day runs are skipped; coupon trues up as of the coupon date.
  3. coupon entitlement was trade-date holdings at the open of the coupon date: now face settled before the coupon date
     (`entitledFace`); a seller flat on trade date keeps a coupon it still owns (task stays scheduled).
  4. previewed settlement amount rounded principal+accrued together; now each to the cent like the fill (`economics`).
  5. `terms.minDenomination` validated and described; `web/lib/contracts.js` bond schema got a "Minimum denomination (face)" field
     (the engine honoured the term but the form could not enter it).
  6. a trade settling on or after maturity was accepted (a matured bill could be bought at its stale quote and was redeemed
     at par at once): `bond.tradeRefusal`, called from one added line each in `server/core/packages.js` (preview) and
     `server/core/orders.js` (fill). Found by treasury_bill.
- `test/core/bond-accrual.test.js`: 6 regression tests for the above (pass).
- `treasury_bill` (Treasury owner, discount, cash settlement stated on the ticket, redemption at par): passes at all three levels.
- Harness: step action `owner_screens` (bond driver `actions.owner_screens` + no-op in lib/actions.mjs + README row) reads the
  Accounting screens in Treasury's scope and compares with the API; `drivers/browser.mjs` screen check no longer expects
  Treasury-owned positions/holdings on the Account's screen.
- `$SP/BUGS.md`: bond prices displayed to 3 decimals (shared `fmtPrice`), not fixed.
- Harness (additive): `lib/normalize.mjs` preview legs carry `accrued`; `lib/actions.mjs` `order.settle` passed when a step states it;
  `specs.test.js` audit: cash = principal + stated accrued, and `settlementCheck: false` on a step skips the standing-lag date check;
  `drivers/browser.mjs`: Positions table accrued is read and compared (API vs screen, spec vs screen).
