# l1: loan family (cash loans and deposits) product tests

Workspace: $SP/l1 (results in results/, sandboxes in tmp/, exploration scripts in x/). Ports 8930-8939 (none in use).
Files I own: test/matrix/specs/loan.mjs, test/matrix/drivers/browser/loan.mjs, test/core/loans.test.js,
the `loan` plugin in server/products/financing.js.

Commands (always with SDT_MATRIX_RESULTS=$SP/l1/results SDT_MATRIX_TMP=$SP/l1/tmp):
  SDT_MATRIX_PRODUCT=unsecured_loan node --disable-warning=ExperimentalWarning --test test/matrix/engine.test.js
  npm run -s test:api -- --product=unsecured_loan
  SDT_MATRIX_SHOTS=$SP/l1/shots npm run -s test:browser -- --product=unsecured_loan   (SDT_LOAN_DEBUG=1 prints what the loan screen reader compared)

## Done
- unsecured_loan: 19 steps (16 passed, 3 blocked as stated) at engine, API and browser. Account-owned borrowing with the
  three views asserted at every step (balance.account, balance.treasury + oversight, balance.book), daily accrual,
  Set rate, part repayment, over-repayment refused, repayment without cash refused, return to / fund from Treasury,
  early repayment in full.
- Browser driver test/matrix/drivers/browser/loan.mjs: Cash loan or deposit ticket (Account page and Treasury page),
  Convert currency and Borrow and convert tickets (written, not yet exercised), Repay/Withdraw (part amount typed in
  the preview), Set rate, transfers from the Account page, New Account, capital. Its `screens` reads the Treasury
  page (own / Account-originated / Book totals / lending), Treasury cash, each Account page and the Book balance
  sheet, against the API and the spec.
- Terminal fixes:
  1. server/products/financing.js (loan.close, loan.open): a part repayment (and an addition to an open
     arrangement) now accrues interest to its date on the principal outstanding until then. Before, the days up to
     a part repayment were later charged on the reduced principal. Regression test: test/core/loans.test.js.
  2. server/core/packages.js (estimateLegs, ARR_CLOSE branch): a `repay` leg is checked at the amount asked for, so
     repaying more than is outstanding is refused ("Outstanding principal is ...; cannot repay ...") instead of
     being cut down silently. Regression test in the same file.
- Harness changes (all additive):
  - lib/actions.mjs: step action `repay` (lot, amount?), `create_account` (name, as), `deposit` takes `withdraw: true`;
    `contractAs` on a trade step keys the instrument a contract leg registers (learnContracts, exported);
    ctx.extraUnits for further Accounts.
  - lib/normalize.mjs: pnl.treasury, balance.treasury, oversight { accountBorrowings, treasuryOwn, accounts };
    positions[].carrying/pledged/onLoan; borrowings[] origin, principalRc, costToDateRc, feesToDate, lender, rateType,
    rateText, dayCount, startDate, maturity, term, collateral, schedule, nextPaymentType, blocked; lifecycle[] and
    openOrders[] carry owner and reason; preview legs carry dailyCost, ccy, financing {...}.
  - lib/specs.mjs: `instruments: {}` is allowed when `tradedOn` says where the contract is entered.
  - specs.test.js: audit rules for `loan` and `repay` preview legs.
  - drivers/browser.mjs: previewAndConfirm takes `edit` (change the legs in the dialog, then re-check) and `inspect`;
    a family may export `screens(ui, ctx, t, info)`; a ledger-carried position's value cell is compared with its
    signed `carrying`; open orders and lifecycle items on the Account screens are counted for the Account only.
- npm test: 224 of 224 (222 + my 2) at the time of the first milestone.

- term_deposit: 13 steps (12 passed, 1 blocked as stated) at engine, API and browser. Treasury, GBP, ACT/365, FX 1.26 -> 1.285
  (reporting-currency income and FX effect by hand), early part withdrawal, stated maturity on Good Friday paid Tuesday 7 April.
- Terminal fix 3: server/core/packages.js (estimateLegs, ARR_OPEN branch): "interest a day" in the preview now uses the
  contract day count (ACT/365 divided by 365; it always divided by 360). Regression test in test/core/loans.test.js.
- loan.mjs `screens` also reads Treasury's pending lifecycle items (Accounting, Pending trades, Treasury scope).

- certificate_of_deposit: 13 steps (10 passed, 1 blocked, 2 unsupported as stated) at all three levels. Account-owned, two CDs,
  one to maturity across Good Friday and Memorial Day, one redeemed early in full; 30/360 refused; secondary sale refused.
- bank_deposit: 14 steps (13 passed, 1 unsupported as stated) at all three levels. Open-ended, monthly interest on the first
  business day, Set rate, part and full withdrawal, second Account (create_account) and Account-to-Account transfer.
- Terminal fixes 4-6 (regression tests in test/core/loans.test.js):
  4. financing.js normalizeRate (loan only): a day count other than ACT/360 / ACT/365 is refused instead of silently becoming ACT/360.
  5. packages.js resolveLeg: a buy/sell trade leg on a loan, repo or securities-loan instrument is refused in words
     (it crashed with "plugin.settleDate is not a function", a 500 at the API).
  6. packages.js growLegs: resizing up a strategy that holds only arrangements says why it is refused (it said "too small").
- loan.mjs driver: ticket kind `secondary_sale` (the arrangement's drawer from Instruments offers no ticket).

- cash_loan (round 2026-10-13): 20 steps (17 passed, 3 blocked as stated) at engine, API and browser. Treasury borrows
  360,000 EUR with Borrow and convert (sold at the bid, value date 7 April over Good Friday / Easter Monday), dollars not
  transferable and euros committed before the value date (both refused), Convert currency back (value 9 April), repayment
  refused on 8 April for want of settled EUR, repaid in full 9 April; the Account lends 150,000 USD (cash_loan) to maturity.
- New: test/matrix/drivers/browser/fx.mjs (minimal: contractTerms for a currency pair; c1 owns the fx family and may
  extend it). drivers/browser.mjs registerInBrowser: Trading currency filled only when the form enables it (a pair's is
  its quote currency, disabled). loan.mjs driver picks a pair by id. web/views/treasury.js pairOptions: two pairs with
  the same symbol are labelled with their names.

- secured_loan: 16 steps (14 passed, 2 blocked as stated) at engine, API and browser. Account buys 5,000 HRDG, borrows
  120,000 pledging 4,000 (pledge of 6,000 refused), selling 2,000 refused (only 1,000 free), 1,000 sold, monthly
  interest, maturity repayment FAILS visibly for want of cash (failed lifecycle item + funding.failed alert, interest
  keeps accruing), Treasury funds, the next cycle repays, shares released and sold.
- Terminal changes for it: web/views/treasury.js ArrangementDialog: "Collateral position" and "Quantity to pledge" for a
  secured / securities-backed loan (sent as terms.collateral; Preview disabled until chosen). financing.js loan.normalize:
  a secured loan with nothing pledged is refused (regression test in test/core/loans.test.js). accounting.js borrowing
  register: loan collateral described "HRDG: 4,000 pledged" (was "4000 HRDG").
- Harness: lib/actions.mjs `$pos:<lot>:<instrument>` in a step's input names an open position (resolvePositions).

- securities_backed_loan: 16 steps (14 passed, 2 blocked) at engine, API and browser. Treasury, SOFR + 1.50% floating
  (fixture fixings), pledge 8,000 BWTU, daily accrual across a reset, fixings stop -> accrual stops, row flagged "no
  fixing since", the 1 April interest payment BLOCKED with its reason, repayment refused, SOFR entered by hand (Data
  connection, Manual entries) -> payment made, repaid in full, shares released, sale settles after Good Friday.
- margin_loan: 16 steps (14 passed, 2 blocked) at engine, API and browser. Security ticket "Borrow the shortfall" (margin
  loan leg sized to the shortfall), unpledged shares sold, repayment before the sale settles refused, part repayment,
  Set rate, monthly interest, repaid in full.
- Terminal fixes for these (regression tests in test/core/loans.test.js):
  7. financing.js loan: a floating loan's interest payment / maturity repayment waits (task blocked, reason shown in the
     register and Pending) while a fixing is missing, instead of paying the interest accrued so far; checkClose refuses
     any repayment while a fixing is missing (a part repayment would charge the missing days later on less principal).
     Maturity now accrues to its as-of date before checking (it accrued to today first: wrong for a late maturity).
  8. packages.js estimateLegs: the preview asks the feed for the reference-rate fixings a floating loan or repo needs
     (it reported "no fixing" for a rate the feed publishes; a repayment preview after a restart would have too).
  9. accounting.js register: a securities_backed_loan is typed "Securities-backed loan" (was "Secured loan").
- Harness: equity.mjs ticket fills "If cash is short" / "Loan rate" from step.financing; loan.mjs: margin path through
  the equity ticket with learnContracts, manual_rate (swap driver's path), the "no fixing since" flag compared with the API.
