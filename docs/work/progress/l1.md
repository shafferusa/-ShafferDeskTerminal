## Done
- unsecured_loan: 19 steps (16 passed, 3 blocked as stated) at engine, API and browser. Account-owned borrowing with the
  three views asserted at every step (balance.account, balance.treasury + oversight, balance.book), daily accrual,
  Set rate, part repayment, over-repayment refused, repayment without cash refused, return to / fund from Treasury,
  early repayment in full.
- Browser driver test/matrix/drivers/browser/loan.mjs: Cash loan or deposit ticket (Account page and Treasury page),
  Convert currency and Borrow and convert tickets (written, not yet exercised), Repay/Withdraw (part amount typed in
  the preview), Set rate, transfers from the Account page, New Account, capital. Its `screens` reads the Treasury
  page (own / Account-originated / Book totals / lending), Treasury cash, each Account page and the Book balance
  sheet, against the API and the spec.
- Terminal fixes:
  1. server/products/financing.js (loan.close, loan.open): a part repayment (and an addition to an open
     arrangement) now accrues interest to its date on the principal outstanding until then. Before, the days up to
     a part repayment were later charged on the reduced principal. Regression test: test/core/loans.test.js.
  2. server/core/packages.js (estimateLegs, ARR_CLOSE branch): a `repay` leg is checked at the amount asked for, so
     repaying more than is outstanding is refused ("Outstanding principal is ...; cannot repay ...") instead of
     being cut down silently. Regression test in the same file.
- Harness changes (all additive):
  - lib/actions.mjs: step action `repay` (lot, amount?), `create_account` (name, as), `deposit` takes `withdraw: true`;
    `contractAs` on a trade step keys the instrument a contract leg registers (learnContracts, exported);
    ctx.extraUnits for further Accounts.
  - lib/normalize.mjs: pnl.treasury, balance.treasury, oversight { accountBorrowings, treasuryOwn, accounts };
    positions[].carrying/pledged/onLoan; borrowings[] origin, principalRc, costToDateRc, feesToDate, lender, rateType,
    rateText, dayCount, startDate, maturity, term, collateral, schedule, nextPaymentType, blocked; lifecycle[] and
    openOrders[] carry owner and reason; preview legs carry dailyCost, ccy, financing {...}.
  - lib/specs.mjs: `instruments: {}` is allowed when `tradedOn` says where the contract is entered.
  - specs.test.js: audit rules for `loan` and `repay` preview legs.
  - drivers/browser.mjs: previewAndConfirm takes `edit` (change the legs in the dialog, then re-check) and `inspect`;
    a family may export `screens(ui, ctx, t, info)`; a ledger-carried position's value cell is compared with its
    signed `carrying`; open orders and lifecycle items on the Account screens are counted for the Account only.
- npm test: 224 of 224 (222 + my 2) at the time of the first milestone.

## Next (in this order; each engine, then API, then browser)
1. term_deposit: Treasury, GBP, ACT/365, matures on a UK bank holiday (Good Friday 3 April 2026 -> Tuesday 7 April),
   principal plus interest returned, reporting-currency figures and FX effect (GBP/USD fixture), early withdrawal.
   Check the preview's "interest a day" uses the contract day count (packages.js divides by 360 always: likely bug).
2. certificate_of_deposit: Treasury, USD, ACT/360, held to maturity; early withdrawal allowed with its figures.
3. bank_deposit: Account, open-ended, monthly interest on the first business day, top-up as a second record, part
   withdrawal, Set rate, Account-to-Account transfer (second Account via create_account), full withdrawal.
4. cash_loan: Treasury borrows EUR and converts (Borrow and convert), T+2 on the joint calendar; liability, interest
   start, available to trade / withdraw before and after settlement; lending side (cash lent); repayment needs
   settled EUR. Needs an EUR/USD pair: no fx browser driver exists (drivers/browser/fx.mjs), decide how to register.
5. secured_loan: Account pledges shares (ticket has NO collateral field yet: add it to ArrangementDialog in
   web/views/treasury.js); pledged quantity cannot be sold; monthly interest; released on repayment.
6. securities_backed_loan: floating (reference rate fixture + spread), missing fixing must block visibly (plan: loan
   plugin blocks interest payment / maturity / full repayment while a fixing is missing; Set rate by hand resolves).
7. margin_loan: bought on margin from the security ticket (financing mode loan) or the loan ticket; open-ended.
8. revolving_credit_facility (partial): draw, part repay, redraw (a second record), commitment fee by hand; refusals.
9. Unsupported day count (30/360): refuse visibly instead of coercing to ACT/360 (normalizeRate, loan only).
10. Full npm test, final report.
